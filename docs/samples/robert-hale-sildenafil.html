Equipment

  • Graduated cylinder

Steps
  1. Calculate: 2.5 mg/mL × 728 mL = 1820 mg Sildenafil. Batch is 728 mL so whole tablets give exactly 2.5 mg/mL; dispense 725 mL.
  2. Gather 91 × Sildenafil 20 mg tablets (1820 mg).
  3. Place the tablets in the mortar and crush to a fine, uniform powder.
  4. Add a small amount of Suspending vehicle + sucrose syrup (1:1) and levigate into a smooth, lump-free paste.
  5. Add more vehicle in portions (geometric dilution), mixing well after each addition.
  6. Transfer to a calibrated amber bottle, rinse the mortar with vehicle, add the rinse, then add vehicle to a final volume of 728 mL (q.s.).
  7. Cap and shake well for 1 minute.
  8. Label and store: room temperature.
