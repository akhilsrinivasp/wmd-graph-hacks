Compounding procedure

Ingredients
  • 10 × Amlodipine 5 mg tablets (50 mg)
  • Suspending vehicle + sucrose syrup (1:1) q.s. to 50 mL
Equipment
  • Mortar and pestle
  • Graduated cylinder
  • Amber plastic bottle
  • Oral syringes
Steps
  1. Calculate: 1 mg/mL × 50 mL = 50 mg Amlodipine.
  2. Gather 10 × Amlodipine 5 mg tablets (50 mg).
  3. Place the tablets in the mortar and crush to a fine, uniform powder.
  4. Add a small amount of Suspending vehicle + sucrose syrup (1:1) and levigate into a smooth, lump-free paste.
  5. Add more vehicle in portions (geometric dilution), mixing well after each addition.
  6. Transfer to a calibrated amber bottle, rinse the mortar with vehicle, add the rinse, then add vehicle to a final volume of 50 mL (q.s.).
  7. Cap and shake well for 1 minute.
  8. Label and store: refrigerate.
Quality checks
  • Uniform appearance, no clumps
  • Final volume checked
  • Re-disperses easily on shaking
  • Second person verifies calculation
Label text
