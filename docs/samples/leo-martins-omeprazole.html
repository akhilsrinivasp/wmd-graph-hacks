Compounding procedure

Ingredients
  • 13 × Omeprazole 20 mg delayed-release capsules (260 mg)
  • Sodium bicarbonate 8.4% vehicle q.s. to 130 mL
Equipment
  • Mortar and pestle
  • Graduated cylinder
  • Amber plastic bottle
  • Oral syringes
Steps
  1. Calculate: 2 mg/mL × 130 mL = 260 mg Omeprazole. Batch is 130 mL so whole delayed-release capsules give exactly 2 mg/mL; dispense 125 mL.
  2. Gather 13 × Omeprazole 20 mg delayed-release capsules (260 mg).
  3. Open the capsules and empty the pellets into the mortar.
  4. Add a small amount of Sodium bicarbonate 8.4% vehicle and levigate into a smooth, lump-free paste.
  5. Add more vehicle in portions (geometric dilution), mixing well after each addition.
  6. Transfer to a calibrated amber bottle, rinse the mortar with vehicle, add the rinse, then add vehicle to a final volume of 130 mL (q.s.).
  7. Cap and shake well for 1 minute.
  8. Label and store: refrigerate.
Quality checks
  • Uniform appearance, no clumps
  • Final volume checked
  • Re-disperses easily on shaking
  • Second person verifies calculation
Label text
  • Give 4 mL by mouth once daily. Shake well before use. Keep in the refrigerator.
  • Discard 30 days after the compounding date (beyond-use date).
  • Compounded medication. Keep out of reach of children.
