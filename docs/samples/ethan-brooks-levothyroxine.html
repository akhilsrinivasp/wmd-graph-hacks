Compounding procedure

Ingredients
  • Levothyroxine USP 9.2 mg
  • Microcrystalline cellulose fill, hypromellose (vegetarian) capsule to 18400 mg total blend
Equipment
  • Analytical balance
  • Mortar and pestle
  • Capsule filling machine
  • Capsule shells (size 3)
Steps
  1. Calculate for 92 capsules (90 + 2 extra): 0.1 mg × 92 = 9.2 mg Levothyroxine.
  2. Dose is under 1 mg per capsule, so weigh an aliquot: mix 100 mg Levothyroxine USP with 9.9 g filler (1:100 trituration), then use 0.92 g of this mix.
  3. Weigh filler (from Microcrystalline cellulose fill, hypromellose (vegetarian) capsule) to a total blend of 18.4 g.
  4. Mix drug and filler by geometric dilution until uniform.
  5. Fill 92 capsules (size 3) using the capsule machine or punch method; target 200 mg each.
  6. Weigh 10 capsules; each must be within ±10% of target weight.
  7. Package 90 capsules, label and store: room temperature.

  • Second person verifies calculation
