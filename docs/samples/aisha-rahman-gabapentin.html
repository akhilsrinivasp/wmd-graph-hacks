Compounding procedure

Ingredients
  • 62 × Gabapentin 300 mg capsules (18600 mg; 18500 mg needed)
  • Starch-based sugar-free suspending base (paraben-free) q.s. to 185 mL
Equipment
  • Mortar and pestle
  • Graduated cylinder
  • Amber plastic bottle
  • Oral syringes
Steps
  1. Calculate: 100 mg/mL × 185 mL = 18500 mg Gabapentin.
  2. Gather 62 × Gabapentin 300 mg capsules (18600 mg; 18500 mg needed).
  3. Open the capsules and empty the pellets into the mortar.
  4. Add a small amount of Starch-based sugar-free suspending base (paraben-free) and levigate into a smooth, lump-free paste.
  5. Add more vehicle in portions (geometric dilution), mixing well after each addition.
  6. Transfer to a calibrated amber bottle, rinse the mortar with vehicle, add the rinse, then add vehicle to a final volume of 185 mL (q.s.).
  7. Cap and shake well for 1 minute.
  8. Label and store: refrigerate.
Quality checks
  • Uniform appearance, no clumps
  • Final volume checked
  • Re-disperses easily on shaking
  • Second person verifies calculation
Label text

  • Discard 35 days after the compounding date (beyond-use date).
